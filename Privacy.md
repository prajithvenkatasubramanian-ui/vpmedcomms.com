We retain enquiry details only for as long as necessary to handle your request or fulfill any resulting professional engagement, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

## 6. Your Rights Under UK GDPR
You have the right to:
- Request access to the personal data we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request erasure of your personal data
- Withdraw consent or object to processing at any time
- Lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at https://ico.org.uk
