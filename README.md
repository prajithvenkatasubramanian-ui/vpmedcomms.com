# VP MedComms Agency — Official Website

Static website for **VP MedComms Agency**, providing medical communications consulting and engagement strategy services to translate complex clinical and scientific evidence into clear, audience-appropriate communications for healthcare and scientific audiences.

## Capabilities Featured
- Expert Insights
- Medical Writing and Publication Strategy
- Creative Scientific Solutions
- Medical Content Management

## Files Included
- `index.html` — Single-file responsive website (Tailwind CSS, GSAP, Lenis smooth scroll)
- `404.html` — Custom error page
- `PRIVACY.md` — UK GDPR Privacy Notice
- `robots.txt` & `sitemap.xml` — Search engine indexing files
- `.nojekyll` — Disables Jekyll processing on GitHub Pages

## Connecting the Google Form
1. Create a Google Form with the fields: Name, Institutional Email Address, Company, Service Required, Project Details, Budget Range, Timeline, and UK GDPR Consent.
2. While signed in to `prajithkanna@gmail.com`, open the form and go to **Responses → ⋮ (More) → Get email notifications for new responses**.
3. Click **Send → Embed HTML (`<>`)** and copy the `src` URL.
4. Open `index.html` and replace `YOUR_GOOGLE_FORM_EMBED_URL` with your copied URL.

## Deployment (GitHub Pages)
1. Upload all files to the root of your repository.
2. Go to **Settings → Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, select **Deploy from a branch**, choose `main` and `/ (root)`, and click **Save**.
4. To attach a custom domain later, add your domain under **Settings → Pages → Custom domain** (this automatically manages the `CNAME` file).
